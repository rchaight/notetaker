Notetaker remote

Notetaker remote is a private, single-user connector. It lets its owner, Robert Haight, ask Claude (through claude.ai) questions about his own notes, tasks and calendar. Access is read-only: it cannot change, create or delete anything.

Who can use it

Only the owner's Google account. There is no sign-up, and this is not a public service or product.

What sign-in does

Signing in uses Google only to confirm that the person connecting is the owner. It requests the openid and email scopes and nothing else. It does not read your Google mail, files, contacts or calendar.

More information

Read the Notetaker remote privacy policy for exactly what is stored and for how long.
